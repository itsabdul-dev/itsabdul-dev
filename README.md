## About

Third-year Application Development student at **CPUT**, based in Cape Town. I work across these stacks. Spring Boot and Java on the backend, React and TypeScript on the front and I'm most interested in the part where a messy real-world process becomes software that people actually use.

Most of my recent work has been in healthcare software: **PracticeFlow**, a CRM that lets doctors manage their patients and practice, which I led a team of students to build, and **MediTicket**, a team-built clinic management system designed around domain-driven design for a university course.
